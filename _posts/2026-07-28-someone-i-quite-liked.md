---
title: "Someone I Quite Liked"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

In a world where everyone’s fake
Today I met someone real
He is constantly made fun of
At times for fair reason
But I’ve never seen him stoop once
And apologize for his actions

So when he graduates,
I’d like to give him my congratulations
For he is the best of us
Born with the worst mindset
And nurtured unkindly
By his school’s temperature

Dear H, all praise in your endeavors!
I hope you only greatness in the future
This I curse you, no malice whatsoever—
For the truly great need no blessing
They need no tears whatsoever
Only desire between him and victory
Strive and all shall be claimed
All you need is strive
Oh, God! All you need is strive
Strive, for I knew you a day
Yet I see the cloth we are cut from
Dear W, seize the sun!
