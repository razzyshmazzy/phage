---
title: "Overwhelming Justice"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

So doth the rightful world turn
The abundance of my soul;
Held shut in a vise,
Locked tight there inside,
Liquifying as if it were ice.

Sometimes doth the world turn
over in its boiling gyre; and sing
to the symphonic heavens
a heathen’s choir; yet if,
when all the world were silent,
men seek to be chaotic,
then so they are the tyrants
of a collapsing society;
Bruised and brutal beyond
the power of words to convey,
horrid in their vast sepulchers,
in broken disarray.

Here, in the deathly quiet,
so numerous are the victims
of past societies; behold me,
this sickened beast, kept locked
as feed for scavengers
and these rabid beasts;
Life is unworthy, yet
death is unimaginable.

How can we live here
where sound is silent,
where light goes to die—
Where vast and lifeless sculptures
watch us dance, alight?
