---
title: "Pianist"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

These autumn-burned leaves
falling like
a heaving chest
and a question

For the one whose touch
I near not felt, who played
my favorite, whose
fingers plucked
Moonlight strings.

You—there, marbled
and parked amid wheat
with sawdust on your
wings.

You—ivory bust,
with copper crust
Harvard weather worn away;
cracked marble,
gold lines and specked
eyes

I move to touch

You fly away

and leave me staring
into the moonless night,
waiting
