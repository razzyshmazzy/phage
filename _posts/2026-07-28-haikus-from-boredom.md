---
title: "Haikus From Boredom"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

Death departs the earth
Like a fleeting memory—
Symbols of regret.

Hatred among friends
A world enshrouded in flames—
What is burnt, is dead.

In lightless struggle
Is there any sanity?
Why do we still continue?

Frozen sky drips ash
On a land devoid of warmth—
Cold divides the land.

In icy numbness
A world succumbing to frost—
These wounds never heal.

Crimson blood flowing
A slave walks amid the war—
Steps trembling in the cold.

Pale skin succumbing
Emancipated rider—
Painful persistence.

Void swallows the sky
A world dark and fathomless—
A flickering light.

In this agony,
A soul once lost to darkness
Is found in the light.

In twisting darkness
Chaos pervades our senses—
Heaven descending.
