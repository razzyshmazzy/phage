---
title: "Let My Death Outlast Me"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

Let my death outlast me
And impart my legacy
Upon the unceasing earth;
Let it sever the past me
In the act of rebirth—
From that spirit within, see
Whatever form it takes
And let it not be shaken
Nor find wrath incurred;
For that which forever dwells
Upon the unceasing earth
Knows my debt, its length and depth,
And yet, would allow my soul
The freedom to live again:
For though I face death,
I find comfort in knowing,
That my legacy survives
In the minds of those
Whom I so sadly leave behind;
Therefore, my son,
Allow me some tranquility,
And let me speak,
That my body not break
Before I can say this to thee:
Let my death outlast me,
Let it sever the past me
And impart my legacy
Upon my loving family;
And to every generation
That follows the first so fondly,
I entrust to them my lineage
And shall fear no longer
The grip of death’s
Chilling indifference,
Knowing in this ending speech
I have spoken my final word
And taken my final breath
