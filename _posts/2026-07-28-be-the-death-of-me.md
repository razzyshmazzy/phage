---
title: "Be the Death of Me"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

Be the death of me,
Hurry my soul into the sky
Or thrust it beneath the earth
Into the searing pit of lava;
And so hold beneath the sea
The flesh that renders me mortal
And therefore fallible to destruction;
Feeling the waves render me insane
I begin to question whether I will remain
Until the sacred light of day
Illuminates all that is profaned—
Such is yet to be assessed;
And once, and only when
My sentence turns to punishment
I will reach above the waters
And fill my lungs with sulfuric air,
Then submerge beneath the magma
Letting myself be remade;
In the vise of molten slag
My eyes begin to liquify,
My body starts to smolder
And burns to black ash;
Salvation assaults my soul
And so here I wonder, waiting,
Thinking faintly of destruction
But more so its implication
That I need to live a certain way;
And I need not fear the fiery waves
Nor the pain which beggars cremation
Instead, as I look upon my past,
Upon all the actions I’ve enacted
And torn from families their fathers
Husbands from their wives:
I hope;
Tempered by the torturous sea,
I know in my heart,
There is no use in changing;
And so I resign myself to see
What lies for me beyond,
Since perhaps I may be saved
By some good god’s grace
But suppositions can wait,
For I feel my end drawing close;
Nearer and nearer,
Looming beyond my sight,
Its impending horror terrifies me;
I tremble in fear,
Feeling each hair on my naked corpse
Stand to some perverse sense of attention
In preparation for the coming silence;
I feel him—and can feel upon my skin
The bated breath of death
As he plucks my soul from out my flesh;
From out my chest, his pace begins to quicken,
Hastening with the passing minute—

Nearing the end, I feel my soul slowly severed:
I feel my mortal soul begin to waver;
What strength I once wielded
Lava has rendered useless;
Each transgression layers atop the last
And my past consigns me to fate;
I will rest for eternity,
Here beneath the waves
