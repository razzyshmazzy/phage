---
title: "Thus Says Darkness"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

Pending my enslavement to His Majesty of the Raven,
I am still in soul, waiting patiently for entrance to his haven;
To that place years pass in torment, dusky and forlorn,
Where tears lie dormant, eerie blackness gives fear form,
Dull dusk is eternal, white moonlight immortal.

Thus says darkness, deepest friend,
As it consumes the vast gray land
And removes all waking thought from the minds of Man—
All is revealed under the light of the glowing sun;
As the waxing and waning moon sheds to bright-eyed dawn,
Who dares defile the placid statue of Alzalam?

Carved from pallid ivory, gleaming with silver starlight,
Stark in the darkness, contrasting ebony midnight,
Wretched fiends try with all their wretched might
To ruin the ivory statue of Alzalam, whose eyes are pools of absent light,
Whose Raven-wings, hidden from sight,
Are bearers of his darkened life,
Vigor brimming with ghostly light.

Unlike anything called weak, Alzalam is of a cloth, sole and unique;
Darkness precedes him, it concedes to him,
Attuned with darkness, he dives, spreading his wings—
A Raven immune to weakness, fluttering in the abyssal deep;
Whose Raven-wings, grim and gaunt,
Haunt the inflated dreams of the egotistic weak;
Whose Raven-wings, grim and gaunt,
Sing songs of what has been, in ancient shadow-fonts.

Then when wolves, to heaven, howl
In light’s absence, he persists,
Blackened mist shrouds his form soundly
Eyes, in vain, search; yet he rests, unstressed, like this vowel;
Darkness is the perched Raven’s dark cowl,
He is Alzalam, that ever-brooding fowl.
