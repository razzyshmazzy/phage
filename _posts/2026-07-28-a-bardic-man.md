---
title: "A Bardic Man"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

I am no more a bardic man, no more a poet,
Than the abolished bristles of a beard,
Rufling silently upon an inquisitor's blasphemous shears.
There is no sound but me.

And strum!
My hands numb at lyre's embrace,
Plucking oh so readily
At brightly-glowing silvery strings.

Striking these songful wires,
Chords a melancholic blue,
My eyes fill with boundless tune,
And my fingers sing, too.

The tavern glows with boundless life,
A light amidst the bumbling knives,
Where washed away are those feelings that incite strife,
They look at me not with their eyes, not with their ears.

Rest, my song reaches them gently,
Playing along upon the strings of their hearts with synchronizing cues.
Caressing their emotions, their moods,
With vibrancy, the radiant tune exhales song and dance.

While they chatter,
Ale-drunken tankards rising and sighing,
My heart throbs with blissful joy—
As I were an inventor, presenting a new toy.

And in doing would receive a load of candid cheers.
So, graciously, my heart would not quell to that feeling persuading,
Carelessly craving, just heartfelt, Heaven-sent,
Tastefully, like a sweet slice of soft brie.
