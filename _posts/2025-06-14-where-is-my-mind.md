Note: Expect more of a time diary, though I’m sure I’ll forget to put dates. Assume each paragraph is written with a length of time in between.

I am writing this from a hospital bed in UCSF. These thoughts come periodically, so forgive me if they appear simplistic.

I’ve been in and out of sleep, awoken every so often to answer their mental trivia. It’s not bad. In fact, the food’s really good.

I have never been one to succumb. Nor, now, do I feel tragically sickened in my state. A little airheaded, admittedly, but not wronged. How can I phrase it? It’s just life, I guess. That’s how it is.

We live, we die, we have strokes. And when we have those strokes, they’re because of causes we cannot control, or even recognize.

The doctors have been very kind. I am not criticizing their care. I am not even criticizing the world or what it’s done to me.

Here’s a quote from a story I half-remembered and then had to Google. It's the first paragraph from Dagon by H.P. Lovecraft:

*I am writing this under an appreciable mental strain, since by tonight I shall be no more. Penniless, and at the end of my supply of the drug which alone makes life endurable, I can bear the torture no longer; and shall cast myself from this garret window into the squalid street below. Do not think from my slavery to morphine that I am a weakling or a degenerate. When you have read these hastily scrawled pages you may guess, though never fully realise, why it is that I must have forgetfulness or death.*

I don’t want death. But I don’t want to forget either. Even if it hurts. Is that selfish? I guess it is. Sorry. I’m not used to not being able to use my brain. I feel like the guy from Flowers for Algernon. And he dies in the end. I don’t think I will, but then again I didn’t think I would have a stroke either. Heaven’s a jester.

And fear’s the master of everything. But it’s waning. Doctors say I’ll be discharged within the week. I miss my cats. I’ve been thinking about them a lot.

Took a little longer but I’m home. Ember is laying on my lap right now. I’m going to shower and sleep, then I’ll finish writing this tomorrow.

The IV mark on my arm has been itching. And these gray little like fur things are all over my skin because of the glue they used. Water and soap aren’t working but oh well.

My dad and I started going on hikes together. It’s fun. I’ll finish this tomorrow.

I’ve been thinking, if what does not kill you can only make you stronger, then why do I still feel so weak?

Cooler minds prevail, so they say. It’s been a week since I left the hospital. I’m trying to stay cool. Hoping I prevail.
