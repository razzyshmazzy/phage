---
title: "If History Buries Me"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

If history buries me,
know that it is because
I did not capitulate.
Know that it is because
I was indignant.
That it is not because of my words
but the way in which I said them.

If history should bury me
and leave my voice a shattered husk,
do not stand by and watch:
Go and exhaust your lips
with speaking Truth;
Exhaust our ears
with hearing it.

If history should bury me,
it is because I spoke of liberty.
It is because what I knew as justice
was not a child with a scrawny ribcage
in the pangs of hunger.

If history should bury me
beneath its suffocating weight,
I will not stagger, fall, or face erasure.
I will rise
from the charnel clay
to face history in its making.

“What is another trembling pain
on this home of horror haunted?”
It is another scar to be healed.

For truth can be found.
Do not silence its seekers.
We must take Truth
and make it scripture
despite its many opponents—
And if history can bury me,
why can’t it bury you?
