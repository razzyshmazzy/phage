---
title: "We are all the same"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

We are all the same
If not in name then in shared days
In blood that bleeds blue
In eyes of similar hue
And yet our greatest adversary is not rust
But the ones we should drink with and trust

We argue of politics and social cues
We kill our martyrs and shun our heroes
Where the loudest is so often not true
We bolt ourselves to opinions and deem others untrue

Thought is not expression unless it’s shared
Art is art till it’s called unfair
We are what remains of the past and that is dust
The past — where History remains untouched
Where the marks upon our bones bear no rust
Where our lies spread like wildfire
And our days went by hushed

In the common age of today
What we often look past, refusing to realize
Is that our differences are the truest stake
In the hope for society to unify
And fight for a common sake
Ever contrary — for we are not all the same

And so like this we must
Must — as we have risen from that earthen dust
Together, in a bond unbroken by Time’s evil rust
And its whispered secrets which seek internal betrayal of us:
Mend our cracked state — repair it with wood,
Rewrite the prejudice, diminish the negative
Annul ifs and past pretenses, sowing seeds for a common good —
Break open a can of brotherhood and drink to our differences
