---
title: "Auroral Drift"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

He emerged from the vortex of a blizzard, half-covered, still moving. His foot left no print in the snow.

The first step. A delay—the earth moved on without him, tripping as if the body were learning its own distance.

Gathering, frost on skin and off forgotten things: rusted metal, blackened wire, a pane of glass half-swallowed by the snow.

Fingers followed their signal, like echoes of intention.

Machines lay everywhere now. Glass split into pale fragments. A rime-lined doll, stiff with frost. Housings filled with ice, as if ice had learned their shape first.

No sound came from them. Only his glacial breath, and the wind, loosening the horizon.

Then, the sky rearranged: Light descended from infinity, bands stretched across the upper field, slow as turning thought, though no thought remained to turn them.

Azure fire, slithering in above him, through snow settling on his skin.

Half-step, after-step, almost-step.

His walk, a continuous fall. Cold finished what it had begun.

The lower limbs withdrew, folding into their final rest. Then the joints gave way. What remained of him no longer belonged to effort.

Above, the sky began to widen—slow bands gathering toward where his eyes failed to follow.

His heartbeat faltered, then vanished.

Sapphire light was sewn by the aurora into his frozen irises and the vacant, immemorial snow.
