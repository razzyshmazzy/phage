---
title: "Absolute"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

I was praying in the desert with clasped hands
when a pale figure, strange and alien,
approached
and in an alien voice, said:
“I, the lifeless, wander here,
a lifelong traveler. This desert
is bare but happy. Look—”
Such a frail hand, he held it with,
before my eyes—a naked skull;
I said:—“True fiend,
why clasp the decaying?”
He held it higher; a ray of light
glinted off the cranium, and he proclaimed:
“You cannot see what hollowed out
eyes see easily.”
From these red-webbed eyes
red tears down a sandy cheek.
The skull blotted out the sun.
“Do I not feel? I feel, but I turn
feeling to dust. I hold
a crying newborn”—
the skull—“I hear an insult,
as I hear my lover’s voice. Hear me,
this depressed joker; find solace
in your sadness.”
My hands were unclasped,
and there was no longer
a prayer left to end—
only sunlight glinting
off the skull of a newborn.
