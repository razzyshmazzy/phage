---
title: "Betwixt the Twilight Moon"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

Betwixt the Twilight Moon,
The sun careened into view.

Trenches everywhere.

Sleeves pulled,
Hands rubbed,
Festering potholes,
It was all so numb.

But everything was so green.
Everything was so serene.

Plains of mildew,
Grassy sinews,
Tied beneath my feet.
Rustling, brustling,
Beneath my feet.

I laid to rest my dirty shovel
Beside my humble town abode.
And set to work a boiling kettle,
A calming tea,
Jasmine green,
But it was so cold.

Stooping beneath the dark,
As the fire played with my peeling skin,
My pale fingers etched thin:

Sincerely, writ I,
Tracing my meandering touch across the page,
Ink-crossed marks,
Scribbled shapes.

Here, beneath the light,
The careening sun,
Shoulders hung,
Betwixt the Twilight Moon,
Back hunched.

Buried in soft embrace,
Thou hath no case!

Lament,
Placid shovel, lament.
Never will your handle withstand me,
Coffers of steel.
Kneel!

There, where the safe lies,
In the last room, the lowest floor,
The narrowest corridor.

O lamenting shovel,
To open its tight embrace,
Need you, a cellar key.

This is simply,
The with-thought melody:

A Gravedigger's Remedy.
