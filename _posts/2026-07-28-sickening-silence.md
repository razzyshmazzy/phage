---
title: "Sickening Silence"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

There is not a man who stands
Against the world when it falls
But he whose hands are clean
Of the world’s impure laws;
I am such a man!
But my lonesome hand refrains
From change to the global stage—
It knows its country’s place;
Yet, solitary escape means death,
So to staunch the bleeding earth
Shall we not, distinct, converge
Into a single, unified force?
O ego! To you I am well accustomed,
Reason be damned.
Land held in the hands of man
Would rather rally hell,
Than bend beneath a banner
Offered by its predecessor;
It would rather face the end,
Hung from a common maw,
For even global intent cannot bend
The lion country’s law;
In the name of all that binds,
I beg you, therefore, before me,
To hurry formality behind
And see the glory of man combined;
But pity, they say, is heresy:
Equality is the devil’s wick—
And lo, this peace, this blasphemy!
Only fools raise their candlestick.
The silence that follows sickens me,
Man allies with his common enemy
