---
title: "America"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

When the crickets sing their solemn croaks,
voices ringing out in blotted skies.
The clouds huddle over with disgraceful plight,
tyranny on display in the world,
whom shall prevail?
Now, deer cry for democracy and frogs dream of monarchy,
yet no two may remain under veil,
for with the coming of the galleon,
and the sailing of the sloop,
the land has been tamed,
Jamestown, Plymouth, and Salem, too.
