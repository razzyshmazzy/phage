---
title: "Executioner"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

Premature sunlight,
A swarm of giggles roaring,
Laughing, sighing.

One…
One is dying!

As axehead replaces the starting sun,
Time is reluctant,
It… comes undone.

Resolve gushes forth,
Soul squeals,
Crowds full of zeal.

But why?
Why?

Why…?
Who wronged them so?
Was it him? He who fell prey?
He, the only in dismay?
A glint of light catches the reddened steel
Axehead falls
Like twisting timber.
Feel, feel.

Splash — a head joins the gore.
Its blaming eyes rest on me.
Before, before.

Sometimes…
Some… times…
Seeing the bleeding head roll,
All guilty after the blood toll,

I grasp at my neck,
Kept on my shoulders
by a single string of glue.

Shifting,
Shuddering,
Muttering:
“What if mine did, too?”
