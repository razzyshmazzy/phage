---
title: "On Time"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

Time is a mistress, she sows seeds of growth in my fertile heart. When I wake tomorrow, I can rise knowing a new sun has similarly arisen. It is all her doing; she is not so bad as some claim. Really her passage only marks change, and is change not what we all seek? Even those stagnant are only stagnant until death. Time is an engine fueled by nothing. It works on oneself as it does on all, yet I think time has blessed me more than the rest. For I wonder, at times, whether change is what I seek or whether if, in changing, my days will turn bleak. But I have never wondered whether Time is a friend of mine; no, line by line, I never grow meek, nor shall I ever embark with fear.
