title: "Haiku"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

Beautiful leaves
Vibrant, so crimson and green
But what of the trees?

So is light’s trade all
In infinity’s cradle
It saw where dark falls

What such sensation
Gives way to salutation
Undo of nations

Tread carefully you
Enemy! Master sent thee?
Then do rest easy

I permit, fellow
Though your leaves carry yellow
Entrance to my hall

Embellish shellfish
Take a risk, then fish a wish
When true, list a twist

Delicate, fragile
Swift, like a windswept trial
Nimble and agile
