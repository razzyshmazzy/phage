---
title: "In Gold"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

What looms in this limbo
Besides shade and shadow?
The sun is a foreigner here,
An immigrant,
An invasive species.
It doesn’t belong here;
neither do we.

Blesséd is our solace,
In this colossal desolation,
Away from the agonies of life,
For society has chased my joy away,
And replaced it with strife.

In days past, I knew a woman,
A fairy’s incarnation,
A girl, my girl, with dyed hair,
That laugh, and she was quiet,
So quiet
Yet her song drew me in.

For Love who guided us,
Through study dates and
Booked weekends; through breaks
And separations:
Have here my heart,
Because my own Love
Didn’t want it.

Tossed it to the floor.
And it shattered.
And I stood there.
And I bent to pick up the pieces.

But you walked away.

I can try to put my love into words,
but that would be insufficient
I think?

You occupy the majority of my heart.
My love persists;
Your love
Was a life sentence.
