---
title: "Riotous Insanity"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

Herald me eternally, as though I were worth more than my metallic shell,
More than molten steel, and more than myself,
As though time has made iron my legacy
And imbued it with bronze iniquity;
Or set upon it golden reverence,
Enacting my form in a marble bust,
An image unrestrained by any natural thing
But of such a sinister lust that my image is made insane.

And if it were, that the earth were to its core as it is to the sky,
Would its visage be as eerie as my own?
Could I look upon the scene clearly?
When the heaven rests above me,
And the ground pours out below
Should any man desire my death
And his life be made meaningless,
Sentenced to dead existence.

But if heaven made plausible my demands,
And let the earth succumb to itself
Would all its inhabitants, in their dying words,
Condemn me for my absurdity?
Or would they pardon me in my apology
Effectively forgiving my sentencing,
Granting me grace amid my iniquity,
And freeing me from my bondage?

O but I still want for things that I cannot have!
I afford nothing above me, yet would cry:
If any man would be so important in himself
To let fortune fall behind the rest?
To starve on his lonesome, beaten by cruel men,
His dignity shed, chained and enslaved—
To see him desperate, and walk by with a lowered head?

Of love, I will speak,
And be made no more than a martyr—
No less than a hero! In death, a lamenting prince,
Of everything cast before me, I shall admit,
Each I regarded infinitely;
And each, having been exhausted,
Has kept me here sitting silently,
My mind clad in riotous insanity.
