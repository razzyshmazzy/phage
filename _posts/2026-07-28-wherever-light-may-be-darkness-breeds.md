---
title: "Wherever Light May Be, Darkness Breeds"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

There are a sort of man that dreadfully exist. For no matter the endeavors of them or their folk — and what a terrible hell it must be — to have their heads in constant turmoil; minds bothered, thoughts slaughtered, flashing images of hellish pyres. This, even when standing upon the grassy-golden plains of heaven.

Through eyes unsoiled:

Heartfully, I wept.

My boat swayed as it kept.

Dastardly shapes on the ketch.

Pain red like crude send.

The ice wept.

Solemnly my boat slid upon the waters.

Crystallion, it was —

Crystallion of the brightest hue.

Stars shined.

Grime marked my face.

Pale as should be.

And I wept.

Hurtfully, I wept. My ketch stood the test of the rocking waves.

It did not cave, stilled and brave.

Water came aboard the deck.

It hopped on, weeping ever so quietly.

And I wept along with it.

The icy pieces crawled the mast like infection spreading.

How blue the scene was;

How it reminded me of "wept”

My song was sung of a sad moon.

Hanging above my shady head with eyes so bright.

Stars of rainy light.

The mast, icy-veined, swayed quietly on the waves.

Water was still.

The image was silent.

Yet I swore, before the brackish hue I could almost see:

Land, ahead of me.

And my ship wept.

Yearning for a brighter tomorrow.

As it swam desperately to the faraway shore.

Climbing past reefs protruding and rocks saluting. My eyes were not teary, nor my breath weary, nor my gaze merely.

Merely saddened by the bow so strong.

Yet I wept.

I would weep as I approached the iron shore.

Ashen sands waved, brushing upon the vile deck of my ship.

Stray planks stuck into the yellow-grey stuff like tentacles.

Of a vile order.

Of a weeping brand.

My heart lurched with a weep.

It was so honest, so vulnerable.

So prone to emotion.

So prone to parlay.

And thus my boots sunk into the rotten sand.

Immobile, I was not.

Yet I stood, glued to the grain.

A sea of the deepest sand.

Strands of hair rolled off my shoulders.

Breeze lifted them up, and set them down.

Again it would occur. Again I remained still.

Ice crackled in my ears, torching my very bones.

Splintering against my muscles.

Fibers of my being caught upon the morbid strings of a forever-weeping wooden violin.

Tightly woven strings of mortality.

They yearned;

Yearned.

Yearned for a day not spent weeping:

A day I would not seek "wept”

An hour I would not find sadness where relief is to be had.

A minute I would relish in hope.

A second my soul would not mope.

A moment —

A mere moment:

A moment I sought to hold as if it were dough.
