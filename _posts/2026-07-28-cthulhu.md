---
title: "Cthulhu"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

Solemn moonlight,
Flickering torch high above,
Sand crunching beneath my feet.

The waves crash and break
against the beach
Pooling, heating my toes an icy cold.

My flame’s last breath,
a gale takes it,
Smoldering out in darkness.

Only darkness.

The moon is gone
And in its place there lay,
A tentacled-face.
