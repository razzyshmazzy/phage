---
title: "Today Dies"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

Tomorrow will arrive
like a soldier returning
from days abroad;
tomorrow will arrive
even if
today dies.

In the entrance,
making contact—

With a pink
pocket knife,
you betray me,
to follow another.

If you would cast an eye
down to see this poor exhibit,
this sun-dried thing, scalped
beyond recognition
by regret and rejection,
aching.

And is patient
like a dog
waiting for his master
who ignores him.
