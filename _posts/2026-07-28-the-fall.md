---
title: "The Fall"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

Bated breath from out the frame
told the skeleton what good the idol wrought,
what craft the hand, and what nailed foot;
Torn from the sky, manmade chaos
came chasing after Heaven; the skeleton
withdrew, drawing limp,
and dipping down. All at once,
there came a great and ceaseless crowd
upon the holy sand, all crawling for
the fallen corpse affixed upon the cross.

The apocryphal eye of God looked down
on you, impudent mass,
you wretch, you heretic.
There is no cure for those exalted souls
who sicken near the burning firmament
and, drawing ever nearer to the fire,
drink deep the ichor, long to see the choir.
You, men of the aged hate, you sang
of endless wine and Babel towers,
but then the flood—no longer did you sing.
Then, your Icarus—his wing!
You withered, yet embraced
the insatiate vulture-beak to eat,
and drove your knees into the sand,
seven hands clawed
at the gaunt and scrawny chest—
three nails tore apart,
ten fingers in His flesh:
Thousands of pious men
eating of human bread.
