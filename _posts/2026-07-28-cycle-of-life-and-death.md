---
title: "Cycle of Life and Death"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
---

On the avenue where dreams go to die,
Silhouettes aimlessly wander,
Meandering on a lonely night.
When there is present such a bustling,
A rustling, upon that alleyway street,
Then Man shall faithfully ponder.

O God, why must acts of this nature go unpunished?

For blowing harshly was the wind,
Whistling up and down that grimy alleyway street;
Drenched in the reek of pain: a dulled, gray misery.
Is it but in death alone that we face judgment?

How unfair it is that my bones ache,
Splintering like infinite hail—
Raked to the side and tossed away,
As if upon a silvery ideal,
I was but a prisoner of the Worthless jail.

Under divine grace, the earth is rung,
In the soil, a grave shaped.
And within that grave, un-cremated, there lies a boy —
A boy of merely eight.

Juvenile though he was, on the day the Reaper stole him,
Sailing away to the far-off lands above;
Where sadness chased him,
And atrocity bathed him.

Hate is a word quite fond to Man's vocabulary, barking it since the times of yore.

Nearly this, and nothing more.
No, nothing more.
