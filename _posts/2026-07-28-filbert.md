---
title: "Filbert"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

It was early June; tourists spoke of Pompeii,
but this sweat was familiar.

Atop this slender concrete column,
I recognized the San Franciscan smell
of fog: eucalyptus and wet pavement;
dry oxalis crunching beneath my toes
Coit Tower left behind.

Concrete emigration: wood planks woven with fennel.
Sun-filled nasturtium in the gaps, moss on the rail,
soft in my palm, feet descending onto Filbert,
where I could no longer hear the camera shutters.
My bare feet, the grass inheriting
Summer’s warmth.

I laughed.

My own voice—a cherry-headed conure
disappeared between the branches
of a Japanese maple.
