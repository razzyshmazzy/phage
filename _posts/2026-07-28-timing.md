---
title: "Timing"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: junior
---

’Tis a golden morning.

This wonderful sun,
this tree with singing birds,
the river with its fish,
splashing and playing,
and the predators
and the prey—
and then you,
without a smile.

“My forest burned down yesterday,” you say.
“This forest is kinder than the last.”
“But I am not over my forest,“ you say.
“This forest forgives.”
“I would feel disgusting to use this forest,” you say.
“Then use this forest to grieve,” I say.

You walk
into the brush.
