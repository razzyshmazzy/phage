---
title: "Seeping through a Shadow"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

My eyes grow dull gazing upon you.
My muscles sting with feigned clearness.
My limbs wrestle the unrelenting assault of sleep.
I sightlessly see, hurt, distraught.

I writhe in cold blood,
Trickling down my spine amidst the red of my beating veins.
The Sound of Silence freezes the air,
And all is still, maimed in icy despair.

Journeying through the night, restless I stirred.
Eyes drooping, rag-dolled upon the floor.
Legs spread before me in a sort of feared play,
Hear me now, when I say:

That upon that hearse,
My name seldom be carved, even called. Never cursed.
With cloudy drags, puffs of blue snow from my snare,
This matter shall be forgotten in the annals of a most fetishized tomb.
Reaping despair.

As most things are, forever rare.
Mouth agape, voice swirling with turmoil, my words tossed into the night's eye,
“Menace!” shrieked I, fearfully, "Reveal yourself unto me!”
Reap your despair!

There was no sound, save the croaking of an ominous toad,
And a band of crickets, playing betwixt me and the nightly crows.
My hands, trembling, brought the sheet before my teeth.
Drooped in white cover, I found myself waiting silently, very stilly.
Heart beating ever so fervently — so loud! so very, very loud!
But ever so sonorous in my ears, thus I could think of none else,
Of no sounds.

Merely perverse waves of paranoia,
Falsified, perchance; yes, this could be it—
But I wouldn't believe it, for above all else,
When it came to my senses,
There was only faith.
Even before you, a shadow-wrenched Wraith.

Perched upon your withering flame,
Eyes snapping me from someplace,
Lacking all sense of shame.

My bones would snap,
My skin would peel;
When suddenly, my flesh begins to heal,
My blood congeals, and the Wraith reels.

The nexus of my heart burns with flame which mends,
The desire to bend, to rend
The impetus which spelled my end.

Should the dripping curtain to my bleeding heart fall,
I'd drown my sorrows till the morrow in a land so raw.
I have no hopes — no, nothing at all.
Despair, in my eyes, is the treacherous dragon's maw.

Flame's embrace is such a cold thing.
But as daunting as it may seem,
It’s what I wish for—
A smiling dream.

A fleeting thing—
I see it in the scattered light
Escaping from the hall, into my room
Through the door ajar, taking flight.

Blackest shadow slithering, stretching from afar.
So very tall, with eyes emitting such a dusky song.
Clad in black as you slip through the crack,
Mouth drooling, your violating gaze pierces into my back.

A collage of the most matted flesh,
Mangled, tangled — abysmal and grotesque.
Despair raging unchecked, I pale-face and brace
As the ship begins to wretch.

With rum and with gin,
Filled up to the brim
Is the ship that ferries me across the lake,
Molded of wicker.
So thin.

I would never be so dull, as to scuttle that ship's hull—
For then the silver of your snickering blade would flicker;
And heads would roll.

What a disgrace.
Before you, but a thrall;
Suppose I should learn my place
In the throes of Bhaal.
I know you're there, lurking in the shadows;
I know you're there, laughing soundlessly in the darkest corner,
While bedside, restless, rests I, fearfully cowering.
Dreadfully souring.

Skin crawling with rapturous tempt;
Oh, how egregiously you must laugh!
Erupting terribly to your lonesome self with sheets of giggles, warm and wild.
Watching me — watching as my figure stirs like a frightened child.

As embers of spotted light crackle, fluttering upon the sill,
My shadow prances upon the wall, like an idiot, never still.
My voice crackles like a dying flame,
And by now, I am forced to wonder silently:

Ponder violently, have you simply left?
Vanishing without a trace?

Or wonder, in that moment, that very moment,
Wrapped up in Darkness’ tight embrace:
For only then did I learn — learn enough to know when I’m mistaken,
Shuddering as I cried, “Present yourself — your case, foulest Forsaken!”

O God, what a blissful sight!
Or not, for here, there is no light.
Muffled shrieks in this place of blight,
You, that Wraith — but a shadow in the night.

Just look!
Your streaky glances and your ruffled figure!
Your paint drips down.
Dripping down,
Pitter, pitter.

Oh, how with twilight-colored despair you stain my soul a pale shade,
And provoke my burrowing within the sanctuary of piled white sheets till the break of day.
Hatefully for that, among more, I say unto you now with voice ever so shaky:
"Wretch! you grandstanding, tricking sheet of light!
This is what you sought from the beginning, ill-mannered fiend—”

—Or is it not, as I deem?
