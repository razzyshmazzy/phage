---
title: "On A Fiery Lake"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
year: freshman
group: "Never-End"
---

Stranded in dire straits,
Where I claw at first ray
Seeking order in the day;
Finding only destruction in its wake.

The bane of all men
Hither in Never-End;
Clung to a strand of silver,
He withers.

And I watch from my mount
At the three-headed hound that caws every sound;
That beats the drum of spontaneity,
And summons the lashing of Hades.

Hither in Never-End
Where the earth quakes,
Violently,
As the world shakes.

O Hatefully,
For their sakes.
As my heart aches,
Stranded in dire straits;
Clawing at first ray,
In a sea, in a lake
Of fiery make.
