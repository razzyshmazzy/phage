---
title: "The Taste of Heaven"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

I do not seek happiness in the heavens. They do not have that which I want. In fact, their mere existence burrows all desires of science in the hated occult, yet under the guise of beloved lords. Of all heavens, and their gods, each I am blessed to acknowledge, and just as tyrannical to turn away from—is what the zealous shall say. Indifference is my virtue. It always is, and always shall be. Still, I cannot simply watch as my purpose—of which I, alone, am master—is brought beyond my grasp by some unfathomable power. It is this very unfathomability, this universal iniquity, which bars my love. But He prescribes love, they will say, therefore I am meant to feel it. My consent is mandatory, regardless of my opinion. No, then, I will not. I refuse to lend my life as a pawn, to submit. For, in submitting, there arises an admittance of deep fear—and more, regret. It stems from the sought-after sciences, those the eye of church doctrine has, and always shall, look upon in derision and conceit. For these reasons, among more, I take offense to the title of “blesséd” and the absence of merit. So, too, for the same reasons, do I tarry at the gates of the heavens. You will see my shadow thrown upon the door there—then, and forevermore.
