---
title: "The Dainty Faerie"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
group: "Never-End"
---

At the outskirts of the world
The farthest corner, unkempt
A place all nocturnal
A place called Never-End

The Outskirts of the world
At its very end;
The farthest corner told,
Nocturnal and unkempt.

Where drunkards mumble
Drivel of the realms,
Bards who dare travel
Number only in the tens.

To this place
So far beyond mend.
To the domineering vista
Of Never-End.

The metallic stench of rot
Hangs in the air
Nowhere to vent,
And only fools say it's not
A welcoming scent.

Here, knowledge is a keepsake,
A mistake
A remedy all faked,
All jaded in the economic lake.

If only it were theirs!
That splendid knowledge!
These thoughts almighty,
This thinking sightly.

Why, what preposterous drivel!

Knowledge?
The great equalizer?
That binding bridge?

They would never allow it
Not even a lick.
They would never give the chance
No! Not a single glance.
No, not for them,
No, not for them.

For the gem’d!
Those owning heaps of gold,
And those silvery holds,
Perpetuating the ever-unequal societal mold.

In their tipsy satin robes:
Devilish violets
Purples and blues;
These are the gem’d,
And they control the clues.

Feasting on thee,
The woes of thee;
Privileges of the highest order,
All for them:
Hoarders! Hoarders!

But this no more;
No, thee shan't concur
No, thee shan’t agree
For this done to thee.

And tis revealed
As the sun slides back
For all to see;
As day fades to black,
And gold meets steel.

Claim now that
Which belongs to thee;
Of riches begotten,
Stolen from thee.

Now go forth upon the castle:
Charge the throne
Raid the palace
And carve them to bone.

Let bloodlust reign;
Stain that steel!
Be their bane,
Make them kneel.

At once!
Grind them to dust
Make their skulls bust
Thee must, O thee must.

Then while the horizon is ivory,
Ebony moon descent;
Mortar is strewn across the foothill
Aimed at each satin windowsill
On this lunar event.

The iron spams, spasms and rolls
And crashes Cyclopean stone,
Defiling it to the bone;
A smashing heap
Of smoke and debris.

Their shrieks are endless
So haughty up there,
In places once so fair,
So full of riches and boundless fares.

But now they sleep,
Among mortar
In the deep;
Their souls so they weep,
Dribbling down,
That crimson as it seeps
Sinking by each
Wretched
Grand piano key.

Soaring o’er
Lands reborn,
Hands unshackled,
O'er radiance once adjourned.

Ere then a dainty Faerie,
Wading through the deep.
Woken by the myriad
Of mans’ many triumphs and leaps.

He is the messenger,
The dainty sort;
Though existing only in myths,
His image shan't distort.

For up there amidst
The ebony-ivory conclude,
Flutters that sneaky minx of a Faerie,
Far away from the crass and crude.

[[That Faerie whom You are drawn to.
That Faerie whose arms You are falling into.]]

And when his little teeth poke out
Like a foxy treat;
You meet his shimmering smiles,
From your Cyclopean seat.

As if hymning the same tune,
Those dainty little smiles,
Aimed right at You.

Each and every one of them,
Aimed right at You.
