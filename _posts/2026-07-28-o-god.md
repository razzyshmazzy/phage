---
title: "O God"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

O God, can I know
How it feels, to grow old
And behold some young sapling
Whose roots are rough and bold;
Can I polish that stone
Turn his coal to emerald?
And curse the day that dawns
When he leaves me alone

Standing long at the broken house
That he leaves and takes flight and
Flies on his own?

When I covet his company,
Will he leave me behind?
O God, I cry, now he's gone!
Let time wear me down
And love heal my bonds.
