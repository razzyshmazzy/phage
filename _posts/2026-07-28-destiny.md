---
title: "Destiny"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

To every man is his natural destiny,
and to all a natural decline
from the face of predestination
to a fate sublime. So grave
is the suffering he endures
to maintain the natural hatred
of his natural course, and yet
so greater is his desire
to see his bodily likeness
maimed, then hoisted up,
on a crucifix of righteousness,
bleeding and barren.
