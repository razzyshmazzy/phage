---
title: "Utility"
date: 2026-07-28
category: poetry
---

I held my son in one palm,
what was left of him at least,
most of him slipped
through the gaps
in my fingers
and onto the floor.
There was a knock at the door—
the doctor,
here to feed me
my utility
like he fed my son
before me.

A million soles
beneath his boots
were now atop my son—
but his eyes were so kind,
and his lips were not a liar’s,
and his hands were not a killer’s
but a doctor’s
that could return all flesh to dust.
